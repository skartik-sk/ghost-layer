Send a private payment

Your wallet signs it locally; Ghost Layer holds it for a random delay, then broadcasts on a fresh connection.

What happens when you send

  1. Sync through GhostYour wallet catches up in whole 1152-block grid cells, through one Nym exit.
  2. Sign on your machineYour wallet signs the payment. Keys never leave it; Ghost only relays bytes.
  3. Wait a random delayGhost holds the payment for 10–40 seconds, so it can’t be timed against your sync.
  4. Broadcast on a fresh connectionA brand-new connection through a different Nym exit, never the sync one.
  5. MinedYou get the explorer link once it’s in a block (about a minute on testnet).

Amount, sender, receiver and memo are shielded on-chain. The server sees two unrelated strangers at unrelated times.

Prove a payment you sent

Creates a ZecProof that reveals only that one payment: recipient, amount and memo. Your viewing key never leaves this machine.

Verify a ZecProof

Paste a proof someone gave you. Ghost checks it against the chain.
